No middleman

We connect. You communicate.

RepairDesk helps customers discover independent repair businesses. The customer and provider handle the conversation and repair directly.

01 · Discover

Search the public directory and review the provider's self-submitted services and service area.

Safety and responsibility

Keep control of your device.

Never share your phone passcode, OTP, banking PIN or account password. Confirm the shop's physical address and business details. Get the diagnosis, final quote, warranty and handover terms in writing before repair. Customer payments, device custody, repair quality and disputes are handled directly by the customer and independent provider.
