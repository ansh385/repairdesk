How the marketplace works

We connect. You choose.

RepairDesk is the platform between the customer and independent service providers. Providers handle the actual repair and delivery arrangements they agree to.

⌕
01 · CUSTOMER

Find providers

Enter your phone issue and area. Browse available independent providers and compare their services, ratings, price estimates and terms.

⇄
02 · MARKETPLACE

Request a quote

Send a request to the provider you choose. They respond with diagnosis, final price, timing and pickup options.

✓
03 · YOUR DECISION

Approve and proceed

Choose whether to accept. The provider completes the repair under the agreed terms. You pay the provider directly; RepairDesk never handles the repair payment. No work should start before your approval.

Clear roles

RepairDesk platform

Provides discovery, enquiry routing, provider profiles and platform support. It does not claim to employ technicians or own repair facilities.

Customer

FAQs

Questions, answered.

Does RepairDesk employ repair staff?

No. It is a marketplace connecting customers with independent repair businesses.

Can providers register themselves?

They can submit an application. A public listing should go live only after verification. Automatic registration and listing require a backend that is not connected yet.

Can customers choose the provider?

That is the intended marketplace flow. The live searchable directory and provider selection require shared accounts and database functionality; this website currently collects enquiries only.

Is RepairDesk accepting live bookings?

Not yet. The public directory, provider verification, booking records, quote workflow and payments are not live. Do not treat an enquiry as a confirmed booking.

Repair business or repair needed?

Start with an enquiry. No fake provider listings, no assigned in-house technicians.
