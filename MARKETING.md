Website: https://repairdesk-lun8.onrender.com  
Repository: https://github.com/ansh385/repairdesk

## Positioning

**Find your repair partner. Your choice.**

RepairDesk is an independent smartphone repair discovery marketplace concept. It aims to help customers discover repair businesses, request quotes and choose a provider. Repair providers are independent businesses; RepairDesk does not employ technicians, own shops or operate a delivery fleet.

## Pilot offer

- ₹0 customer platform fee.
- ₹0 provider registration fee.
- ₹0 provider subscription.
- ₹0 RepairDesk commission during the initial pilot.
- Customers pay the chosen repair provider directly.
- RepairDesk does not collect, hold, split or transfer repair payments.

Do not promise that enquiry submission, provider registration, bookings, payments or provider listings are live. The current website is a static pre-launch landing page. Its forms do not transmit or store submissions and the provider directory is empty pending real verification.

## Trust and safety rules for all marketing

- Never invent provider names, ratings, reviews, customer counts, repair jobs, response times or coverage areas.
- Never claim the marketplace is fully operational or that bookings are confirmed.
- Never request device passcodes, OTPs, UPI PINs or banking credentials.
- Explain that the repair provider is responsible for the repair and its agreed warranty.
- Do not publish a provider until identity, business details, service capability and consent to listing are checked.
- Do not publish private owner contact information.
