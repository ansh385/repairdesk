# RepairDesk marketing kit

## Positioning
Doorstep-coordinated smartphone repair: pickup, diagnosis-first quote, customer approval before repair, and return delivery. Ahmedabad pilot; service availability must be confirmed by locality.

## Customer WhatsApp message
Hi RepairDesk, I'm interested in the Ahmedabad smartphone repair pilot. Please confirm whether my area and device issue can be served, and share any pickup/diagnostic charges, repair partner details, quote and applicable warranty terms before collection.

## Creator collaboration DM
Hey! We're preparing RepairDesk, a doorstep smartphone repair pilot in Ahmedabad. The customer journey is home pickup, diagnosis and quote, approval before repair, then return delivery. We're looking to speak with local tech creators about an honest pilot walkthrough. Would you be open to discussing a collaboration? No claims of active citywide coverage—we're validating the service area and partners first.

## Content ideas
1. Explain the diagnosis-first quote and approval process.
2. Show a sample device condition checklist and chain-of-custody receipt (use a dummy device, no customer data).
3. Explain how customers can protect personal data before handing over a phone.

## Claims to avoid until verified
- Same-day repairs, guaranteed turnaround, citywide coverage, certified technicians, warranty coverage, fixed prices, customer testimonials, or completed-order counts.
